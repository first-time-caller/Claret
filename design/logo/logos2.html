Claret · logo, round two

The rim, the mark it leaves, and the pour

Two ideas carried forward. The mark: a deep core with a paler rim, the colour of wine in the glass. Its reverse is the stain a glass of red leaves on linen, darker at the edge and pale inside, the mark the wine leaves on you. The word: Claret filled to a pour line with a real meniscus, which becomes the launch animation.

1 · The rim, refined

In the glass

The tilt is now real: the core sits up and left and the rim thins towards one side, as when you tip a glass over a white cloth. A fine outer line is the edge of the bowl.

2 · The mark it leaves

A ring of red on linen

A drying drop of wine pulls its colour to the edge, so the stain is dark at the rim and pale inside. Slightly irregular, never quite closed: proof someone was here, drinking something good.

3 · The pour

Claret, filled to the line

The fill stops at a meniscus that curves up at the edges, deep at the bottom and paler at the surface, like the rim. Filled about a third: a proper pour, never to the brim.

4 · The reveal

The launch, frame by frame

The word is drawn as an empty glass outline, then fills from the bottom in tonight's colour, settles at the pour line with a small wobble of the meniscus, and the mark appears beside it. About 1.2 seconds.
